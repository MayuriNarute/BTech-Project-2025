About Us

Welcome to the Blood Cancer Detection Platform. Our mission is to provide an advanced, AI-powered diagnostic tool that helps in early detection and classification of blood cancers, aiding medical professionals in accurate and timely diagnoses.

Our Vision

We aim to leverage cutting-edge technologies like GANs and CNNs to enhance diagnostic accuracy, support personalized treatment, and provide a user-friendly web application for healthcare professionals and patients.

Our Team

We are a group of passionate developers, researchers, and medical professionals working together to make cancer diagnosis more efficient and accessible.
